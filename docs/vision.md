# Vision — Letters to 2040

## Core question

**What should remain human in 2040?**

Letters to 2040 is a participatory digital experience about the choices we make as technology becomes more capable.

The project is not anti-technology and it is not a prediction exercise. It is interested in the tension between progress and intention: what we automate, what we preserve, what we redefine, and what people want to carry forward.

## Desired outcome

A visitor should leave with:
1. one idea they had not considered before,
2. one perspective from another person they remember,
3. a desire to articulate their own answer.

## What the project is not

- a generic AI-awareness website
- a dystopian manifesto
- an AI-generated gallery pretending to be human testimony
- a technology demo searching for a message
- a survey dashboard

## Audience

The initial audience is broad by design, but research should intentionally include people with different ages, professions, relationships with technology and levels of technical literacy.

## Experience principles

### Human
Real voices, real ambiguity, real contradictions.

### Deliberate
Every interaction should earn its place.

### Accessible
The experience should work across devices and should not rely on sound, motion or visual spectacle to be understandable.

### Honest
Research findings, participant counts and outcomes should never be inflated.

### Evolving
The work should change when better evidence appears.

## Leadership principle

The quality of this project should be visible not only in the final artifact, but in how the team is briefed, how contributors are credited, how decisions are made and how feedback changes the work.
