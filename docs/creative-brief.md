# Creative Brief — Letters to 2040

## Single-minded idea

**The future is not only something that happens to us. Some parts of it are choices.**

## Central prompt

**What should remain human in 2040?**

## Emotional arc

1. **Curiosity** — this feels beautiful and intriguing.
2. **Recognition** — these questions are already part of my life.
3. **Tension** — progress creates trade-offs.
4. **Perspective** — other people see this differently.
5. **Agency** — I have an answer worth contributing.
6. **Hope** — the future is still something people can shape.

## Tone

- thoughtful
- intimate
- contemporary
- warm
- precise
- optimistic without being naive

## Avoid

- dystopian clichés
- anti-AI preaching
- corporate futurism
- empty inspirational copy
- excessive motion
- visual complexity without narrative purpose
- fake statistics
- AI-generated participant quotes

## Visual direction

Editorial and cinematic rather than dashboard-like.

Use:
- strong typography
- generous negative space
- human photography
- tactile imperfections
- restrained motion
- quiet transitions
- visual contrast between human material and technological systems

## Sound

Sound should be optional and additive. The core narrative must remain understandable with audio disabled.

## Interaction

The interface should feel intentional rather than gamified.

Every interaction should answer one of three questions:
1. Does this reveal a perspective?
2. Does this create reflection?
3. Does this help the visitor contribute?

If not, remove it.

## Success

Success is not “the site looks expensive.”

Success is that people understand the question, stay with the narrative, remember at least one perspective and want to answer for themselves.
