## The question

**What should remain human in 2040?**

Letters to 2040 is a living digital experience built from real voices, interviews, ideas, images, sound and participation.

It is not meant to predict the future. It is meant to create a space where people can think about the future together.

The project will explore questions around technology, creativity, attention, relationships, work, identity and the things we may choose *not* to automate.

---

## The experience

The public experience is planned around a simple journey:

1. **Enter** — encounter one central question.
2. **Listen** — move through real perspectives from different people.
3. **Reflect** — discover tensions, patterns and opposing ideas.
4. **Respond** — write your own letter to 2040.
5. **Become part of it** — each contribution becomes part of the evolving installation.

The goal is not endless scrolling. The goal is a short, deliberate experience with a beginning, tension and emotional resolution.

## Project principles

**Human before technological.**  
Technology should disappear behind the experience.

**Real voices before generated sentiment.**  
Participant contributions should remain recognizable as human expression.

**Clarity before spectacle.**  
Motion, sound and visual effects must support the narrative.

**Evidence before ego.**  
When feedback contradicts an assumption, test it.

**Credit the work.**  
Every meaningful contribution should be acknowledged.

## Process

The project is being developed in public through a documented creative process.

### 01 — Research
Interview people with different relationships to technology and identify recurring tensions, hopes and fears.

### 02 — Narrative
Turn research into a coherent story instead of a wall of quotes.

### 03 — Creative direction
Define typography, photography, motion, sound, pacing and interaction principles.

### 04 — Prototype
Build the smallest version that can test whether the story works.

### 05 — Test
Observe real people using it. Measure comprehension, completion and emotional response without pretending subjective experience is reducible to one metric.

### 06 — Iterate
Document what changed, why it changed and what trade-offs were made.

### 07 — Release
Publish the experience and invite new letters.
