# Letters to 2040 — official visual identity

<p align="center"><img src="letters-to-2040-banner.svg" alt="Letters to 2040 white editorial lettering and golden paper plane over deep blue" width="100%"></p>

The visual source is the supplied approved artwork. Its defining elements are white editorial letterforms, a delicate gold flight path and origami paper plane, and a cinematic teal-to-midnight-blue background. The reusable SVGs here are vector adaptations of that artwork; preserve the official source when making future exports.

## Palette

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight navy | `#091C36` | Depth and main interface background |
| Deep ocean | `#0A2D54` | Brand canvas, panels |
| Teal | `#218B9C` | Atmospheric gradient highlight |
| Flight gold | `#E9C17C` | Plane, lines and selected actions |
| Paper white | `#FFFFFF` | Lettering and primary text |
| Mist | `#BBCFD8` | Secondary body text |
| Letter paper | `#F5EFE6` | Letter preview surface |

The gold is a visual thread, **not** a blanket background. Use navy and teal for cinematic depth, restrained serif typography for the story, and warm paper to signal a personal letter. The site should remain readable without animation; screen-reader and reduced-motion support take precedence over effects.

## Usage

- Do not replace the official serif logo with a generic text lockup; do not separate its gold flight path or distort its proportions.
- On a small navigation bar, use the paper-plane mark and readable project name instead of shrinking the entire hero logo to illegible dimensions.
- Preserve the title **Letters to 2040** in both languages. Translate editorial copy and the experience, not the proper name.
- The public prototype does not publish people's letters: drafts remain local to the device. Do not imply that a public archive or interview dataset exists before it does.
